One review queue

The plan’s steady second set of eyes.

Spotting critical waypoints with the precision of a seasoned Master—like having an experienced captain review and validate your route safety every step of the way.

Alert review queueIllustrative only
  1. Dummy CheckI-042 · I-043

    Verify the lowest charted depth between these waypoints before approval.

  2. Safety conditionallyI-064

    UKC meets the rule, but the point remains inside a No-Go area; maintain planned speed and confirm the tidal window.

  3. UnsafeI-065 · I-066

    UKC is below the rule inside a No-Go area; reduce speed, use the best tidal window, and complete the risk assessment.

  4. Potential AgroundI-067

    At or below the Shallow contour; stop approval or execution of the affected section and escalate immediately.

Every alert ties back to a route and waypoint, so review starts with evidence rather than a generic warning.
